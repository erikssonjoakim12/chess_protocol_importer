Free? Safe?

The free tier is enough for reading your games; no credit card is needed. On the free tier Google may use what you send to improve its products.

Treat the key like a password: don't share it. For extra safety, lock the key to this app, so a copied key is useless anywhere else:

  1. Open Google Cloud → Credentials (same Google account) and click your key.
  2. Under Application restrictions choose Websites and add https://erikssonjoakim12.github.io/*
  3. Under API restrictions choose Restrict key and tick Generative Language API. Save.

If you also run the app on your own computer, add http://127.0.0.1:8000/* as well.
